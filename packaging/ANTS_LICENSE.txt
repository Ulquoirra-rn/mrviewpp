This distribution of MRView++ includes the antsRegistration executable from
Advanced Normalization Tools (ANTs), used to align the built-in tract atlas to
the subject being viewed.

ANTs is developed by the ANTsX team and is distributed under the Apache License,
Version 2.0. The full text of that licence is available at:

    http://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

and the ANTs source at:

    https://github.com/ANTsX/ANTs

The antsRegistration binary shipped here is unmodified, taken from an official
ANTs release. MRView++ itself is distributed under the Mozilla Public License,
Version 2.0; the two are separate programs and are merely aggregated here.

If you use the atlas alignment in published work, please cite ANTs:

    Avants BB, Tustison NJ, Song G, Cook PA, Klein A, Gee JC. A reproducible
    evaluation of ANTs similarity metric performance in brain image
    registration. NeuroImage, 2011; 54(3):2033-2044.
